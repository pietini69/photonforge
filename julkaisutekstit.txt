1. REDDIT: r/seestar
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sivusto: https://www.reddit.com/r/seestar/

Otsikko:
Put together a free desktop app for Seestar / smart scopes (Mac, Windows, Linux) - stacks, aligns mosaics, and runs overnight sequences

Teksti:
Hey folks,

Like a lot of you, I've really enjoyed using my Seestar, but I always felt something was missing on the computer side. I wanted an easy way to plan multi-target sessions, control the scope from my desk, and stack the raw FITS frames without having to juggle four different heavy programs or pay hundreds for software licenses.

I ended up writing a native desktop app called PhotonForge in Rust. It's completely free, lightweight (around 10 megabytes), and starts up in less than a second.

What it currently does:
- Controls the telescope over local Wi-Fi / network (targets, altitude planning, live framing).
- Night automation: dither, re-focus checks, and running sequences while you sleep.
- Stacking and mosaic stitching: aligns frames (correcting field rotation from alt-az mounts), handles sigma-clipping, drizzle, and blends mosaic panels smoothly.
- Post-processing: basic background extraction (gradient removal), star color calibration, curves, and star reduction.
- Saves and loads FITS, 16-bit TIFF, XISF, and PNG.
- Supports Seestar (S50, S30 and the new Pro models) as well as DwarfLab (Dwarf 3, Dwarf Mini, Dwarf II) and custom scopes via ASCOM Alpaca.

If anyone wants to try it out on their data or use it for planning:
GitHub (direct downloads): https://github.com/pietini69/photonforge/releases/latest
Gumroad (free, name your price $0): https://shop.pietiniart.com/l/photonforge

It's an ongoing project, so if you run into any quirks or have ideas for things you'd like to see added, let me know. Hope someone finds it useful.

3. REDDIT: r/AskAstrophotography
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Sivusto: https://www.reddit.com/r/AskAstrophotography/

Otsikko:
Built a lightweight native stacking & processing tool in Rust (Mac, Windows, Linux) - free to download

Teksti:
Hi all,

Over the past few months I've been building a desktop tool called PhotonForge. I wanted something native and fast that opens in a second, handles the everyday 90% of stacking and processing, and doesn't cost an arm and a leg or require complex scripting.

It's written in Rust and compiled as a standalone ~10 MB binary for macOS (Universal), Windows 64-bit, and Linux.

Main features included so far:
- Stacking: Sub-pixel star alignment (including rotation matching for alt-az data), sigma-clipping rejection, 2x drizzle, and batch filtering.
- Mosaic stitcher: Automatic registration and seamless cosine blending for multi-panel mosaics.
- Calibration & corrections: Auto-STF stretch, polynomial background gradient removal (ABE), SCNR, and photometric star color calibration (SPCC/PCC).
- Narrowband & color: SHO / HOO / LRGB channel combiner, arcsinh stretching, custom spline curves, and star reduction.
- Built-in plate solver with catalog annotations (Messier, NGC, IC).
- Smart telescope tools: Wi-Fi connection, mosaic planner, and night sequencer for Seestar and DwarfLab scopes.
- File formats: Reads and writes 16/32-bit FITS, PixInsight .xisf, 16-bit TIFF, and PNG.

It is completely free (freeware / open source):
GitHub releases: https://github.com/pietini69/photonforge/releases/latest
Gumroad: https://shop.pietiniart.com/l/photonforge

Happy to hear any constructive thoughts or bug reports if you decide to take it for a spin on your subs.
